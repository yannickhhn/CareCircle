Features

One household, everyone included

Create a household for your family and bring everyone into one shared space: the people you care for and the people who help care for them.

Care together, not alone

Invite co-parents, siblings or other caregivers as co-managers. Everyone sees the same up-to-date information without sharing a login

Everyone you care for

Add your kids, your parents, or anyone who depends on you, each with their own profile inside the household.

Medications for the whole household

Track each member’s meds and doses, and check them off each day so every manager knows what has been taken.

Appointments in one place

Keep every member’s doctor visits and checkups on a shared household list, so nobody shows up on the wrong day.

Doctor and prescription cards

Save each member’s doctors and prescription details so they’re ready when you call the pharmacy.

Free for most families

One household with up to four people costs nothing. Upgrade only if your circle grows

How CareCircle works

Set up your household in few minutes. From then on, every carer stays on the same page.

01

Create your household

Sign up for free and give your household a name, like “The Sun Family”.

02

Invite your co-managers

Add the people who share the caregiving, such as a partner, siblings or a nanny, so everyone sees the same information.

03

Add the people you care for

Create a profile for each kid, parent or dependent, with their medications, doctors and appointments.

04

Stay on top of it together

Check off doses and see upcoming appointments from one shared dashboard, whoever is on duty that day.
